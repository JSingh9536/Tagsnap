# Real ticket photos

Drop 20–50 real scale tickets in `fixtures/tags/`. **That folder is gitignored**
— real tickets carry customer names, job sites, and pricing, and must not end up
in version control.

Deliberately include the bad ones: faded, greasy, folded, torn, shot at an
angle, taken in a dark cab, carbon-copy second sheets, anything with
handwriting on it, and any ticket that ever caused a payment dispute. The messy
ones are the useful ones — that is what drivers actually send.

Phone photos straight off the camera roll are exactly right. Do not crop,
straighten, or clean them up.

## What to measure

Run twenty through the app and count how often **net tonnage** comes out right.
That single number decides how heavy the review step has to be, and it is much
cheaper to learn it now than after the first settlement goes out.
